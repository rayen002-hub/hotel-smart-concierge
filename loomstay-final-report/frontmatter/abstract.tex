\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This work was carried out as part of our final-year project at \textbf{Loomens}, a Tunisian web development and digital transformation agency located at the Cyberparc of Djerba, in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the Professional Master's degree in Software Engineering, awarded by the Higher Institute of Computer Science and Mathematics of Monastir (ISIMM).

\medskip

The \textbf{LoomStay} platform enables hotel establishments to digitalize various services for guests and staff. It covers digital check-in with traveler forms, guest room access via QR code with PWA installability, complaint management with automatic AI classification, multilingual messaging with automatic translation, intervention assignment for maintenance and housekeeping employees, daily cleaning task management, and intervention validation through QR code scanning via a Flutter mobile application.

\medskip

The solution relies on a service-oriented architecture composed of a React/TypeScript web application, a Node.js/Express backend with Prisma ORM and PostgreSQL, a FastAPI AI service integrating the pre-trained NLLB-200 model for translation and a TF-IDF / Logistic Regression classifier for complaint categorization, and a Flutter mobile application for field employees.

\medskip

\textbf{Keywords:} Hospitality, Digitalization, PWA, React, Node.js, Flutter, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Automatic Translation, NLLB-200, QR Code, Scrum.
